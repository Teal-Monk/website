Questions, answered.

What happens after the 10-day trial?

Nothing dramatic. You drop to the free Seed plan automatically — your data stays exactly where it is. Upgrade whenever you're ready, or keep using Seed for as long as you like.

What counts as a "voice hour"?

Time spent recording and transcribing — meetings and voice notes. Seed includes about 2 hours a month (roughly four meetings); Sprout 10 hours; Rainforest 25.

Can I switch or cancel?

Anytime, in a click. Annual is billed once a year; monthly month to month. Cancelling stops the next charge and leaves your data untouched.

Is my data private?

Popy is local-first — your data lives on your Mac, not our servers, and is never used to train AI. See how privacy works →
