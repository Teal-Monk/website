Features

One place for everything you know.

Notes, meetings, articles, digests, tasks, events — all connected.

Capture

Write it. Say it. Record it.

Rich text notes with images and checklists. Voice memos transcribed instantly. Meeting recordings with speaker identification, AI summaries, and action items — extracted as tasks automatically.

  • Auto-detects Zoom, Meet, and Teams
  • Speaker identification in transcripts
  • AI summaries and action items
  • @mention people to build interaction history
Discover

Save anything. Research what matters.

Save & Read

One-click saving from your browser. Full article extraction — not just a bookmark. Read distraction-free, even offline. Track tools and products from "To Try" to "Using."

Digests

Pick a topic. Popy searches the web on your schedule and delivers what's new — cross-referenced against what you already know. Each digest builds on the last.

  • Topics suggested from your saved knowledge, or type your own
  • Highlights what's genuinely new to you
  • Free plan: 1 topic. Sprout: 2. Rainforest: 5. See pricing
Connect

Everything remembers where it came from.

Tasks

Every task knows its origin — which meeting, which article, which note. Created from meetings automatically, or added from anywhere. Due dates, priorities, full context.

Calendar

Google Calendar sync with context from Popy. Before a meeting: attendee history, related notes, open tasks. After: recording and summary linked to the event.

Knowledge Threads

Everything you save becomes connected — by topic, person, and time. An article links to the meeting where it was discussed. A task traces back to the note that inspired it. You don't build this. Popy does.

Your memory. Any AI tool.

Popy speaks MCP — the open protocol for connecting AI tools to data. Claude Desktop, OpenClaw, or anything that speaks MCP can use your knowledge as context.
