Features

One place for everything you know.

Notes, meetings, articles, digests, tasks, events — all connected.

Write it. Say it. Record it.

Rich text notes with images, checklists, and inline tasks. Voice memos transcribed instantly. Meeting recordings with speaker identification, transcription, and AI summaries. Action items are extracted as tasks automatically — linked back to the moment they were discussed.

  • Auto-detects Zoom, Meet, and Teams
  • Speaker identification in transcripts
  • AI summaries and action items
  • @mention people to build interaction history

Research that arrives on schedule.

Pick a topic. Popy searches the web on your schedule — weekly, fortnightly, or daily — and delivers what's new, cross-referenced against what you already know. Each digest builds on the last. Browse your history as a scrollable timeline. Every digest is saved to Popy, growing your knowledge automatically.

  • Topics suggested from your knowledge graph, or type your own
  • Digests highlight what's genuinely new to you
  • Previous digests inform the next — a running narrative on each topic
  • Free plan: 1 topic. Sprout: 2. Rainforest: 5. See pricing

Save anything. Find it later.

One-click saving from your browser. Full content extraction — not just a bookmark, the actual article. Read distraction-free, even offline. Track tools and products you discover. Move them from "To Try" to "Tried" to "Using."

  • Chrome extension saves with one click
  • Distraction-free reader with offline support
  • Auto-tagging and folder suggestions
  • Everything searchable — even articles from months ago

Tasks that remember where they came from.

Every task knows its origin — which meeting, which article, which note. Weeks later, you'll know exactly why you added it.

  • Extracted automatically from meeting summaries
  • Create from notes, articles, or conversations
  • Due dates, priorities, tags
  • One tap to see full context

Walk into every meeting prepared.

Google Calendar integration with context from your knowledge graph. Before a meeting: attendee history, related notes, open tasks. After: recording and summary linked to the event.

  • Automatic Google Calendar sync
  • Meeting prep with relevant context surfaced
  • Post-meeting recording and summary attached to the event
  • Contact interaction history across meetings, notes, and tasks

The layer underneath everything.

Every feature in Popy is powered by connections between your content. An article and a meeting about the same topic are linked. A person in a note connects to every meeting they attended. A task traces back to the discussion that created it. You don't build this. Popy does. It's what makes search smart, digests personal, and AI integrations powerful.

  • Search understands relationships — find a person and see every meeting, note, and article connected to them
  • Digests know what you know — they cross-reference your saved knowledge
  • AI tools get structured context — typed, tagged, timestamped, and connected

Your memory. Any AI tool.

Popy speaks MCP — the open protocol that connects AI tools to your data. Claude Desktop, OpenClaw, or anything that speaks MCP can use your knowledge graph as context. You control what each tool can see.
